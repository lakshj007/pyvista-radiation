# Controlled patch-antenna sensitivity experiments

These small archived outputs are regenerated by `simulations/run_sensitivity.py`
in the solver Docker image; `examples/sensitivity_report.py` analyzes them without
a solver. Full time-domain files remain ignored under `simulations/work/`.

| Case | Resolution | Substrate cells | Boundary / variation |
|---|---|---|---|
| mur20 | λmin/20 | 4 | Baseline Mur |
| mur25 | λmin/25 | 6 | Finer patch edge and spatial mesh |
| mur30 | λmin/30 | 8 | Finest spatial mesh in this experiment |
| pml20 | λmin/20 | 4 | Eight-cell PML; NF2FF box moved inside absorber |
| large20 | λmin/20 | 4 | Box dimensions multiplied by 1.5 |
| double20 | λmin/20 | 4 | Doubled excitation; adaptive termination diagnostic |
| fixed20 | λmin/20 | 4 | Unit drive; exactly 60,000 steps |
| fixed_double20 | λmin/20 | 4 | Doubled drive; exactly 60,000 steps |

Each NPZ contains raw complex `e_theta` and `e_phi`, ordered
(frequency, signed theta, phi), with frequencies 2.2/2.4/2.6 GHz, signed theta
−180:2:180° and phi 0/90°. These raw fields retain the pulse Fourier spectrum
(V·s/m). `prad_raw` has the corresponding W·s² convention. `dmax_planes` is the
solver's maximum over these sampled planes, not an independently sampled full
sphere. The `port` columns are frequency Hz, Re/Im S11, raw incident/accepted
spectral powers, and Re/Im single-sided excitation spectrum (seconds).

For unit drive, divide the raw complex fields by the source spectrum and the
drive multiplier; divide raw power by the squared magnitude of the same factor.
`report.json` records calibrated powers, apparent radiation efficiency and the
controlled comparisons. No clipping is applied to apparent efficiencies; values
slightly above one expose numerical error. The reference `patch_reference.npz`
is separately calibrated with its own archived source waveform.

The finest pair has less than 0.223% RMS cut-amplitude change, but its complex
S11 change exceeds 0.02. The declared combined refinement criterion therefore
fails. This is useful sensitivity evidence, not a convergence or measurement
claim. Inspect the exact logs and XML, rather than inferring behavior from the
nominal case labels. Fixed-window controls must both show 60,000 actual iterations.

Generated GPL case sources with retained attribution are in
`simulations/sensitivity_sources/`; numerical outputs here follow `data/LICENSE`.
